EGF binds its receptor, the receptor phosphorylates itself and the adaptor GRB2:GAB1, and the adaptor stimulates PI3Kα. PI3Kα makes PI(3,4,5)P3 from PI(4,5)P2. PTEN removes the 3-phosphate and returns PI(4,5)P2, and the 5-phosphatases (SHIP1/2, SYNJ1/2, INPP5B, INPP5F) make PI(3,4)P2, which PTEN and INPP4A/B dephosphorylate further. The level of PI(3,4,5)P3 is set by its synthesis against two routes of removal, and how fast it follows a change of input depends on the removal (Ferrell 2022, sections 6.1 and 6.2).
