LKB1 with STRAD and MO25, or CaMKKβ with Ca2+ and calmodulin, phosphorylates the α subunit of AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK), and the phosphatases PP2A and PP2C remove the phosphate. AMP stimulates the phosphorylation and inhibits the dephosphorylation, acting on both enzymes of the cycle in the same direction (Ferrell 2022, section 5.15). Active AMPK phosphorylates HMG-CoA reductase (HMGCR), PP2A reverses it, and the unphosphorylated enzyme makes mevalonate from HMG-CoA.
