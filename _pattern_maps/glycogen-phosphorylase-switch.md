Phosphorylase kinase phosphorylates glycogen phosphorylase, and phosphorylase phosphatase removes the phosphate. When the two enzymes work close to saturation, the share of phosphorylated phosphorylase changes steeply over a narrow range of kinase activity, a switch built from enzyme saturation without cooperativity. The book's evidence is reconstituted muscle enzymes: the response was sigmoidal at 70 µM phosphorylase, a few times above the enzymes' KM values, but not at 20 µM, and phosphorylase may reach 100 µM in skeletal muscle.

## Source

**Book.** Ferrell 2022, sections 5.6 and 5.7, zero-order ultrasensitivity. The book's example is the phosphorylation of muscle glycogen phosphorylase by phosphorylase kinase against phosphorylase phosphatase, in reconstituted enzymes: the response was sigmoidal at 70 µM phosphorylase, a few times above the enzymes' KM values, but not at 20 µM, and phosphorylase may reach 100 µM in skeletal muscle.

**Added for the drawing.** The identifiers: PYGM for the muscle phosphorylase, PP1 (PPP1CA, PPP1CB, PPP1CC) for the phosphatase, as the MRegAtlas has them. Phosphorylase kinase, a complex of four subunits, stays one box as the book names it, without identifiers.
