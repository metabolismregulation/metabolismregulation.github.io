Sos activates Ras by exchanging its GDP for GTP. Active Ras-GTP binds Sos and activates it allosterically, so Sos bound to Ras-GTP activates more Ras. Each product of the step makes the step faster, a short positive feedback loop.

## Source

**Book.** Ferrell 2022, sections 1.9 and 13.1, positive feedback in the EGFR system, with Ras and Sos as the example of a short loop (among the positive loops of section 16.1).

**From the book in this map.** Sos, Ras with GDP and with GTP, Ras-GTP binding Sos, and Sos bound to Ras-GTP activating Ras. Drawn new for this page. Sos is drawn twice, as the catalyst and as the partner of Ras-GTP. Bound GDP and GTP are drawn as complex members, as on the MRegAtlas, the free nucleotides left out.

**MRegAtlas.** No MRegAtlas map draws Sos. The Pattern Atlas map [Incoherent feed-forward](/pattern-atlas/#egfr-sos-gap-incoherent) shows Sos and GAP on the same Ras step.
