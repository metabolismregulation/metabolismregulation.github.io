Caveolin-1 (CAV1) binds endothelial nitric oxide synthase (eNOS) at the plasma membrane. Ca2+ bound to calmodulin joins the CAV1:eNOS complex, HSP90 binds next, and CAV1 leaves, giving eNOS with calmodulin and HSP90. The binding partners that activate eNOS displace the one that holds it, so the inhibitor sets how much activator is needed before eNOS is freed (Ferrell 2022, section 4.5).
