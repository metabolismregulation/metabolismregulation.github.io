The catalytic subunit of PKA phosphorylates hormone-sensitive lipase (HSL) and the complex of HSL with perilipin 1 (PLIN1), which then releases HSL. HSL hydrolyses triacylglycerol to glycerol and fatty acids. PKG1 bound to cGMP stimulates both phosphorylations. Each kinase reaches lipolysis by two routes of the same sign, the arrangement of coherent feed-forward regulation, which can make the response more switch-like (Ferrell 2022, section 5.14).
