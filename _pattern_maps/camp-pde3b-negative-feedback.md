cAMP binds the regulatory subunit of PKA and frees the catalytic subunit. PKA-C phosphorylates the phosphodiesterase PDE3B, and phosphorylated PDE3B hydrolyses cAMP to AMP, so cAMP speeds up its own destruction. Insulin signalling reaches the same step: PI(3,4,5)P3 recruits PDPK1 and AKT, and AKT phosphorylates PDE3B too. Negative feedback of this kind makes the steady state more stable and lets the system respond faster (Ferrell 2022, sections 11.1 and 11.2).
