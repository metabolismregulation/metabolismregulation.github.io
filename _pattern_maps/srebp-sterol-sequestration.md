SCAP escorts SREBP2 from the endoplasmic reticulum to the Golgi in COPII-coated vesicles. In the Golgi, the proteases S1P and S2P cleave SREBP2, and its released fragment enters the nucleus with importin-β. Sterol and INSIG bind SCAP:SREBP2 and hold it in the endoplasmic reticulum, so cholesterol turns off the activation of the factor that drives its own synthesis. A binding partner that holds a regulator in an inactive complex must be titrated before the regulator acts (Ferrell 2022, section 4.5).
