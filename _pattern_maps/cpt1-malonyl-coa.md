Carnitine palmitoyltransferase 1 (CPT1) makes acyl-carnitine from acyl-CoA and carnitine, the step by which fatty acids enter mitochondria for oxidation. ACACB makes malonyl-CoA, which binds CPT1 and blocks it. AMPK, activated by LKB1 and stimulated by AMP, phosphorylates ACACB, and PKA phosphorylates LKB1. The inhibitor here is a metabolite, made and removed by the cell, so the signals act on CPT1 by changing how much of its inhibitor there is (Ferrell 2022, section 4.5).
