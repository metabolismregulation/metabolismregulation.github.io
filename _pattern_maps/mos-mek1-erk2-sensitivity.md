Each tier of a cascade can make the response steeper. In Xenopus oocyte extracts the MAP kinase cascade turns a graded Mos input into a switch-like ERK2 output: the Hill exponent is about 1.7 for MEK1 and about 5 for ERK2. Ultrasensitivity in the tiers multiplies through the cascade, and the multisite phosphorylation of MEK1 and ERK2 contributes to it. A cascade can lose sensitivity too, where a tier saturates.

## Source

**Book.** Ferrell 2022, sections 7.2 to 7.5, sensitivity amplification in cascades, with the Xenopus oocyte MAP kinase cascade.
