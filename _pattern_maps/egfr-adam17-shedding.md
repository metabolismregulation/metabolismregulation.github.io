The sheddase ADAM17 cleaves the membrane-bound precursor of TGFα and releases active TGFα, which binds and activates the EGF receptor. Active EGFR in turn leads to the activation of ADAM17, so each protein activates the other. The loop reinforces EGFR activation and spreads it to neighbouring cells.

## Source

**Book.** Ferrell 2022, section 1.9, positive feedback in the EGFR system, with EGFR, ADAM17 and TGFα as the example of a long loop.

**From the book in this map.** The TGFα precursor, ADAM17, TGFα, EGFR and the active receptor stimulating the shedding. Drawn new for this page.

**Not in the book, so not drawn.** How EGFR activates ADAM17, which the book says is not worked out (PKC is a likely intermediary). The active receptor therefore acts on the cleavage itself. The spread to neighbouring cells is not drawn.

**MRegAtlas.** No MRegAtlas map draws ADAM17 or TGFα. A positive feedback is drawn in [Eicosanoids](/atlas/#eicosanoids), where Ca2+ stimulates its own release.
