Epinephrine binds the β2-adrenergic receptor, which exchanges GDP for GTP on Gsα. Gsα bound to GTP activates adenylate cyclase, which makes cAMP. cAMP frees the catalytic subunit of PKA, PKA phosphorylates phosphorylase kinase, phosphorylase kinase phosphorylates glycogen phosphorylase (PYGM), and phosphorylated PYGM breaks glycogen down to glucose 1-phosphate. Each enzyme of the chain acts on many molecules of the next, so a few hormone molecules move a large amount of glycogen, and a chain of enzymes can reach an abundant target faster than one enzyme alone (Ferrell 2022, section 7.1).
