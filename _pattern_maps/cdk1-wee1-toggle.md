Two proteins that inhibit each other form a double-negative feedback loop, which works like a positive one: either can win. Cdk1 inactivates Wee1 by phosphorylation and Wee1 inactivates Cdk1 by phosphorylation, on similar time scales. With enough ultrasensitivity the pair is bistable, with Cdk1 on and Wee1 off, or the reverse, and an input moves it from one state to the other through a saddle-node bifurcation. Two mutually repressing transcription factors are the transcriptional version of the same switch.
