A kinase phosphorylates a protein and a phosphatase removes the phosphate, so the protein cycles between two forms and its steady state is set by the balance of the two enzymes. With both enzymes working far from saturation, the response to the kinase is graded, Michaelian. Activating the kinase or inhibiting the phosphatase, or both at once, moves the balance, and the same cycle with saturated enzymes is the basis of zero-order ultrasensitivity.
