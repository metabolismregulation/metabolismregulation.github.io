## Description

LTB4 binds two receptors, the high-affinity BLT1 (LTB4R) and the low-affinity BLT2 (LTB4R2). BLT1 couples mainly to Gi and also to Gq-family proteins, whose α subunit activates phospholipase Cβ, so IP3 and calcium rise. In sensory neurons BLT1 sensitises TRPV1, while BLT2 opposes that sensitisation.
