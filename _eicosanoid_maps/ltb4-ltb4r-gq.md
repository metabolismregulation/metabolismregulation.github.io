## Description

LTB4 binds two receptors, the high-affinity BLT1 (LTB4R) and the low-affinity BLT2 (LTB4R2). In sensory neurons BLT1 sensitises TRPV1 and carries the thermal hyperalgesia caused by LTB4, while BLT2 opposes that sensitisation. BLT1 couples mainly to Gi and also to Gq-family proteins, which raise IP3 and calcium. 5-lipoxygenase, upstream of LTB4, drives joint inflammation in a mouse model of osteoarthritis.

## Colour

Each block is coloured by its effect on pain, red where it raises pain and blue where it relieves it, and everything in it takes that colour, GTP and GDP included, as it works for the block's result. Drugs are yellow and classes of molecules white.

This block is red: LTB4 recruits neutrophils and sensitises nociceptors.
