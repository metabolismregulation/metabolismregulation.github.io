## Description

Prostacyclin (PGI2) formed in inflamed tissue binds the IP receptor on DRG nociceptors, a Gs-coupled GPCR that activates adenylate cyclase and raises cAMP. The resulting PKA activity, together with PKC, sensitises TRPV1 and lowers its heat threshold toward body temperature. In the rat monoiodoacetate OA model IP antagonism reduces joint pain, placing prostacyclin alongside PGE2 as a peripheral mediator of OA pain.

## Colour

Each block is coloured by its effect on pain, red where it raises pain and blue where it relieves it, and everything in it takes that colour, GTP and GDP included, as it works for the block's result. Drugs are yellow and classes of molecules white.

This block is red: PGI2 raises pain in inflamed tissue.
