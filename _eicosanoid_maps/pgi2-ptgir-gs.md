## Description

Prostacyclin (PGI2) binds the IP receptor (PTGIR), a Gs-coupled receptor that activates adenylate cyclase and raises cAMP. In sensory neurons PKA, together with PKC, sensitises TRPV1 and lowers its heat threshold.
