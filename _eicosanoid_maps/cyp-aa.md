Cytochrome P450 epoxygenases, CYP2J2 among them, turn arachidonic acid into the four epoxyeicosatrienoic acids, 5,6-, 8,9-, 11,12- and 14,15-EET, and soluble epoxide hydrolase (EPHX2) turns them into dihydroxy acids (DHETs). ω-Hydroxylases of the CYP4A and CYP4F families make 20-HETE, and CYP2E1 and CYP2J2 make 19-HETE. 16-, 17- and 18-HETE are other hydroxylation products.
