## Description

PGE2 binds the EP2 receptor (PTGER2), which couples to the G protein Gs. GTP replaces GDP on Gsα, which leaves the receptor and activates adenylate cyclase, so cAMP rises. Gsα then hydrolyses its GTP by itself and joins Gβγ again.
