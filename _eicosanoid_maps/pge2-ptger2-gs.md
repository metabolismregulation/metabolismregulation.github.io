## Description

PGE2 binds the EP2 receptor (PTGER2), which couples to the G protein Gs. GTP replaces GDP on Gsα, which leaves the receptor and activates adenylate cyclase, so cAMP rises and sensitises the neuron. Gsα then hydrolyses its GTP by itself and joins Gβγ again. In nociceptors EP4 carries most of the sensitisation by PGE2, while EP2 acts mainly in the spinal dorsal horn, where it reduces inhibition.

## Colour

Each block is coloured by its effect on pain, red where it raises pain and blue where it relieves it, and everything in it takes that colour, GTP and GDP included, as it works for the block's result. Drugs are yellow and classes of molecules white.

This block is red: EP2 raises cAMP and sensitises nociceptors and dorsal horn neurons.
