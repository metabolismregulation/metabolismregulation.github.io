Free radicals oxidise arachidonic acid without an enzyme into isoprostanes such as 8-iso-PGF2α and 8,12-iso-iPF2α-VI, markers of oxidative stress. 8-iso-PGF2α is broken down to 2,3-dinor-8-iso-PGF1α.
