Lipoxins are made by two lipoxygenases acting in turn, often in two cells. ALOX5 turns 15(S)-HETE and 15(S)-HpETE, made by ALOX15, into lipoxin A4 and B4, the latter through the epoxide 5,6-epoxy-15(S)-HETE. ALOX12 and ALOX15 turn LTA4, made by ALOX5, into the same epoxide, and ALOX12 turns it into lipoxin A4 and B4. Aspirin-acetylated COX-2 makes 15(R)-HpETE, from which ALOX5 makes the aspirin-triggered 15-epi-lipoxins. HPGD inactivates lipoxin A4 to 15-oxo-LXA4. The epoxide also gives lipoxin C4, which leaves the cell and is turned into lipoxin D4 and E4 as the cysteinyl leukotrienes are.
