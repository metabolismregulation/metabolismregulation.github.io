## Description

Mast cells make mainly prostaglandin D2 (Boyce 2007). PGD2 binds the DP1 receptor (PTGDR) on DRG neurons, a Gs-coupled GPCR. In cultured DRG neurons of adult rats, DP1 raises the tetrodotoxin-resistant Na+ currents through NaV1.9 and NaV1.8 and lowers the threshold of the action potential; DP2 on the same neurons counteracts it, and the net effect of PGD2 is pronociceptive (Ebersberger 2011). Mast cell PGD2 sensitises visceral C fibres through DP1 (Zhang 2013), and DP1 activates airway sensory nerves (Maher 2014). For the joint the evidence comes from cultured neurons, without a pain test in animals. In the spinal cord DP1 acts the other way and dampens pain.

## Colour

Each block is coloured by its effect on pain, red where it raises pain and blue where it relieves it, and everything in it takes that colour, GTP and GDP included, as it works for the block's result. Drugs are yellow and classes of molecules white.

This block is red: through DP1, PGD2 sensitises DRG neurons.
