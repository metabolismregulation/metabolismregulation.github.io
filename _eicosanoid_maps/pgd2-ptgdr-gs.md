## Description

PGD2, the main prostanoid of mast cells, binds the DP1 receptor (PTGDR), which couples to Gs: Gsα activates adenylate cyclase and cAMP rises. In sensory neurons DP1 raises the tetrodotoxin-resistant Na+ currents through NaV1.9 and NaV1.8, while DP2 on the same neurons counteracts it.
