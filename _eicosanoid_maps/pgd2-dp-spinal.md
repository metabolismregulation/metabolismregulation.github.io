## Description

In the spinal cord PGD2 inhibits allodynia through DP receptors (Minami 1997). After nerve injury, activated spinal microglia make PGD2 through cyclooxygenase 1, and this PGD2 causes mechanical allodynia through DP2 on spinal neurons (Kanda 2013).

## Colour

Red raises pain, blue relieves it, and olive acts both ways. Each molecule is coloured by its role in this block.

DP1 is blue and DP2 red. PGD2 is olive, as it acts both ways.
