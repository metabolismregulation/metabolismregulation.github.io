The 12- and 15-lipoxygenases ALOX12 and ALOX15 oxygenate arachidonic acid to 12(S)- and 15(S)-HpETE, and 12(R)-HpETE is made as well. Glutathione peroxidases (GPX1, GPX4) reduce the hydroperoxides to 12(S)-, 12(R)- and 15(S)-HETE, and CYP4F enzymes hydroxylate 12(S)-HETE at carbon 20. 12(S)-HpETE rearranges into the hepoxilins A3 and B3, which are hydrolysed to trioxilins. 15(S)-HpETE gives eoxin A4 (14,15-LTA4), from which eoxin C4, D4 and E4 are made as the cysteinyl leukotrienes are, and soluble epoxide hydrolase (EPHX2) makes a 14,15-diol. Lipoxygenases acting one after the other make the 5,12- and 5,15-diHpETEs and diHETEs, and ALOX12 and ALOX15 oxidise 5-oxo-ETE further.
