## Description

PGE2 binds the EP1 receptor (PTGER1), which couples to Gq. Its α subunit activates phospholipase Cβ, so IP3 and calcium rise, and RGS proteins end the signal by accelerating GTP hydrolysis. In sensory neurons this pathway sensitises TRPV1 through protein kinase C and enhances calcium entry through store-operated Orai1 channels.
