## Description

PGE2 formed in the inflamed joint binds the EP1 receptor, which about a third of sensory neurons express. EP1 couples to Gq, whose α subunit activates phospholipase Cβ. In sensory neurons this pathway sensitises TRPV1 through protein kinase C and lowers its heat threshold, and it enhances calcium entry through store-operated Orai1 channels. Mice lacking EP1 show less PGE2-induced allodynia and inflammatory heat hyperalgesia. RGS proteins end Gq signalling by accelerating GTP hydrolysis. The role of EP1 in osteoarthritis pain is inferred from these inflammatory pain models, while blocking EP4 relieves pain in an osteoarthritis model.

## Colour

Each block is coloured by its effect on pain, red where it raises pain and blue where it relieves it, and everything in it takes that colour, GTP and GDP included, as it works for the block's result. Drugs are yellow and classes of molecules white.

This block is red: EP1 raises calcium in nociceptors.
