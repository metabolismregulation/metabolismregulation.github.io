## Description

Several lipoxygenase products, 12(S)- and 15(S)-HpETE, 5(S)- and 15(S)-HETE and LTB4, open the capsaicin receptor TRPV1, and hepoxilin A3 opens TRPV1 and TRPA1. The cyclopentenone prostaglandins 15d-PGJ2 and PGA2 and the epoxide 5,6-EET activate TRPA1. Through both channels calcium enters the cell. Both channels are best known on sensory neurons, where their opening excites the neuron.
