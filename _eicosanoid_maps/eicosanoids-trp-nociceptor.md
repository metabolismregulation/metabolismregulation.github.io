## Description

Several lipoxygenase products, 12(S)- and 15(S)-HpETE, 5(S)- and 15(S)-HETE and LTB4, open the capsaicin receptor TRPV1 of sensory neurons (Hwang 2000), and hepoxilin A3 opens TRPV1 and TRPA1 (Gregus 2012). The cyclopentenone prostaglandins 15d-PGJ2 and PGA2 cause pain through TRPA1 (Materazzi 2008), as does 5,6-EET at the central terminals of sensory neurons (Sisignano 2012). Through both channels calcium enters the nociceptor.

## Colour

Red raises pain, blue relieves it, and olive acts both ways. Each molecule is coloured by its role in this block.

The mediators and both channels are red. 15d-PGJ2 is olive, as it also relieves joint pain (Pena-dos-Santos 2009).
