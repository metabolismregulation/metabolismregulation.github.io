Phospholipase A2 (cPLA2α and cPLA2γ) releases arachidonic acid from membrane phospholipids. Three groups of enzymes turn it into eicosanoids. Cyclooxygenases (PTGS1 and PTGS2) make prostaglandin G2 and H2, from which prostaglandin, prostacyclin and thromboxane synthases make the prostanoids. Lipoxygenases (ALOX5 with FLAP, ALOX12, ALOX15, ALOX12B and ALOXE3) make hydroperoxy- and hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acids, the leukotrienes, the lipoxins, the eoxins and the hepoxilins. Cytochrome P450 enzymes make epoxyeicosatrienoic acids, which soluble epoxide hydrolase (EPHX2) turns into diols, and the ω-hydroxylated HETEs. Further enzymes inactivate many of the products, among them 15-hydroxyprostaglandin dehydrogenase (HPGD), the prostaglandin reductases and the ω-oxidation of leukotriene B4. Aspirin-acetylated COX-2 makes 15(R)-HETE, the precursor of the aspirin-triggered lipoxins.
