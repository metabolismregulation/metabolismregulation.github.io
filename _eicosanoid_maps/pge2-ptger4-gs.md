## Description

PGE2 binds the EP4 receptor (PTGER4), a Gs-coupled receptor that activates adenylate cyclase and raises cAMP. In sensory neurons the cAMP drives PKA-dependent sensitisation of channels such as TRPV1 and NaV1.8.
