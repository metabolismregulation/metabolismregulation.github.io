## Reactome-Recon connection

The connection was chosen from the <a href="/reactome-recon-proteins/">Reactome-Recon lists</a>: proteins of the Recon2 metabolic reconstruction that take part in Reactome signalling processes. CPS1 is in the lists through the Reactome processes <a href="https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9955518">SIRT5 desuccinylates CPS1</a> (R-HSA-9955518) and <a href="https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9955504">SIRT5 deglutarylates CPS1</a> (R-HSA-9955504).

## Description 

The urea cycle converts ammonia into urea in the liver. Its rate-limiting step, the synthesis of carbamoyl phosphate by carbamoyl phosphate synthetase 1 (CPS1) in mitochondria, needs N-acetylglutamate, made from acetyl-CoA and glutamate by N-acetylglutamate synthase (NAGS). Carbamoyl phosphate then enters the cycle through ornithine transcarbamylase. Succinyl and glutaryl groups on CPS1 lysines lower its activity. The mitochondrial sirtuin SIRT5 removes them and activates the enzyme, and mice lacking SIRT5 have higher blood ammonia.
