The list the atlas viewer is expected to show, with every layer it needs. The numbers are estimated processes: from the pieces drawn so far and the reactions in the sources, about a third to a half of a source count ending up drawn. The default view is by enzyme branch (structure). Where the view by family (branching) puts a block elsewhere, the block says so. The same atlas in eight layers is on the layers page.

Five layers, three of them in the list as it works now (group, item, tab). The domain is a new heading above the groups, and the block lives inside the map: picked from the list it highlights its part of the map, and it is what the family view is assembled from. Maps and tabs hold 2 to 43 processes, median 8. 15 blocks change home in the family view.
