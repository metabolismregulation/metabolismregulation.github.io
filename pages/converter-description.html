The Converter turns a map from one format into another. It runs in the browser, and the files never leave the computer. The annotation (HGNC, UniProt, ChEBI) goes with the map. In GraphML it is read from the node properties, or from the node links when a map has none.

From GraphML

The map is first checked against the drawing rules. Elements that break them are left out of the converted files and listed under the map, one per line with the element's id, label and reason. The red button "GraphML (marked)" gives the map with them drawn red, the reason shown on hover in yEd.

  • CellDesigner: two files, with straight-line edge routing (yEd's name) and with advanced routing.
  • GraphML (corrected): a map drawn like SBGN but with other shapes comes back in the SBGN palette. The shapes are recognised by their outline. The file is given only when the map needs it.
  • SBGN-ML.

From CellDesigner

  • GraphML: the map drawn in our yEd conventions.
  • GraphML (advanced): the CellDesigner drawing kept.

From SBGN-ML

  • GraphML and the two CellDesigner files, each made directly from the SBGN-ML. Glyphs the converter does not know are left out and listed under the map.

Standard node sizes

With this box ticked, the GraphML made from CellDesigner or SBGN-ML gets the standard sizes: proteins 80 x 40, metabolite circles 36 x 36, metabolite stadiums sized to the name. A metabolite keeps its shape. The map is rescaled if its objects are much larger or smaller.
