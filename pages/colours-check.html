Colours: map check

The map of the Colours page is the yEd export below, with a role on every fill and text. These views show that the roles leave the drawing unchanged and are given correctly.

Original

The yEd export as uploaded.

Tagged, in the original colours

The Colours page's map with each role set to the colour it has in the original. It should look exactly the same.

Difference

The two above laid over each other, at full size (shrunk, the browser draws an SVG image slightly differently from the same SVG in the page). White where they are identical, any difference in colour.

Roles

One colour per role. Each text takes a darker shade of the role it was given, so a text in another hue than its fill sits on the wrong role.
