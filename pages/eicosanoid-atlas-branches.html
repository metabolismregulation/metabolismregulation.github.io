The atlas organised by branching, as the pathway resources group the eicosanoids: a family of mediators is a block, and each branch in it starts at a branch point, a first oxygenation product such as PGH2 or LTA4, and runs to the mediator, its receptors and its end. The other proposal, by enzyme branch and fatty acid, is on the structure page, and the list as the viewer would show it on the list page.

The tree

From the membrane phospholipids and the fatty acids they release, through the branch points to the branches, grouped into families, with the receptors at the end. Numbers: reactions in the sources (KEGG, Reactome, Rhea, Recon3D, MetaCore, the AsthmaMap and the OA map) sorted by the names of their products, 721 of 751 sorted, the rest left over. Red dashed lines: a branch with a second root in another family.

Would it work?

What works

  • Readers find things where they expect them. Reactome (Prostaglandins and thromboxanes, Leukotrienes and eoxins, the specialized pro-resolving mediators by family), MetaCore (Prostaglandin 2, Leukotriene 4) and reviews all group this way.
  • A branch is one story. Synthesis, mediator, receptor and inactivation sit together: the PGE2 branch holds mPGES-1, the EP receptors, the exporter ABCC4 and HPGD. With enzyme branches the receptors are a separate block.
  • Prostanoids and leukotrienes are clean trees. One root each (PGH2, LTA4) and one synthase per mediator, so the branches are the natural parts and the 57-reaction prostanoid piece cuts itself.
  • A tree lays out well. Each branch grows from its root, the shape the growing layout makes.
  • Fatty acids stay as tabs for the prostanoids and leukotrienes (PGE1, PGE2, PGE3).

What does not

  • Hydroxy acids are not a branch. 12-HETE comes from ALOX12, from cytochrome P450 and from radicals, and 20-HETE only from P450. The family is a structure class (Reactome splits it by carbon position).
  • Two roots. Lipoxins need 5-LOX and 15-LOX, often in two cells, and eoxins are 15-LOX leukotrienes. They belong to two branches at once (red dashed lines).
  • Enzymes repeat. ALOX15 sits in 15-HETE, eoxins, lipoxins, D-resolvins and protectins. HPGD ends prostaglandins, lipoxins and resolvins. A drug or a gene is scattered: needs an enzyme view on top (a colour mode).
  • Sizes differ a lot. The DHA mediators have about 100 reactions, isoprostanes a handful.
  • Fatty acids drift apart. EPA products are tabs in prostanoids but a family of their own in resolvins. The side-by-side comparison of AA with EPA, the grid of the other page, is lost.

The list: families and branches

Two levels: the family, then its branches. A fatty acid is a tab of a branch where the chemistry is the same. File names put the family first: prostanoids-pge2, leukotrienes-ltb4, a fatty acid last where needed (prostanoids-pge2-epa).
