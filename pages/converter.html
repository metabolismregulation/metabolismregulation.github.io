Map converter

Drop one or more yEd GraphML maps drawn with the SBGN palette. Each map comes back as two CellDesigner files:

  • direct: a basic, reliable conversion with straight lines, to be finished by hand in CellDesigner.
  • advanced: process squares and modifier arcs placed as in yEd, so the layout already looks good in CellDesigner.

The conversion runs in your browser. The files do not leave your computer. The annotation (HGNC, UniProt, ChEBI) comes from the map's node properties. A map without them gets the ChEBI and UniProt links of its nodes.
