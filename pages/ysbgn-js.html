ysbgn.js converts yEd GraphML maps drawn with the SBGN palette to SBGN-ML, and SBGN-ML or Newt maps to GraphML. The files keep the layout of the input map. Straight lines layout is a fallback option. The conversion runs in the browser, and the files never leave the computer.
