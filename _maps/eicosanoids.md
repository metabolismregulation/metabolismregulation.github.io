## Reactome-Recon connection

The connection was chosen from the <a href="/reactome-recon-proteins/">Reactome-Recon lists</a>: proteins of the Recon2 metabolic reconstruction that take part in Reactome signalling processes. cPLA2 (PLA2G4A) is in the lists through the Reactome processes <a href="https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-111898">Phosphorylation of cPLA2 by ERK-2</a> (R-HSA-111898), <a href="https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-428961">Phosphorylation of cPLA2 by MAPK p38 alpha</a> (R-HSA-428961) and <a href="https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-111881">Phospho-cPLA2 translocates to membranes when intracellular calcium levels increase</a> (R-HSA-111881).

## Description

Cytosolic phospholipase A2 (cPLA2α, PLA2G4A) releases arachidonate from membrane phosphatidylcholine, the first step of eicosanoid production, and cyclooxygenases turn arachidonate into prostaglandin G2. The enzyme needs a rise in cytosolic Ca2+ and phosphorylation. In platelets, thrombin cleaves and activates its receptor PAR1, which activates Gαq. Gαq stimulates phospholipase Cβ, which splits PI(4,5)P2 into IP3 and diacylglycerol, and IP3 opens the IP3 receptor to release Ca2+ from the endoplasmic reticulum. Ca2+ binds the C2 domain of cPLA2α and brings the enzyme to its membrane substrate. Phosphorylation at Ser505 by ERK1/2 or p38α keeps it on the membrane at low Ca2+. At rest the annexin A2:S100A10 complex holds cPLA2α off the membrane. MNK1, activated by ERK1/2 and p38α, phosphorylates Ser727 and frees the enzyme. Pyrrophenone is a selective cPLA2α inhibitor.
